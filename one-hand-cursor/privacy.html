One-Hand Cursor Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

One-Hand Cursor (Android package com.evmodu.onehandcursor) is published on Google Play by Dev Jang. The app provides a user-controlled floating touchpad and cursor to make one-handed screen interaction easier. It does not require an account.

AccessibilityService Use

One-Hand Cursor uses Android AccessibilityService only after the user reviews an in-app disclosure, explicitly agrees, and enables the service in Android settings. The service displays the floating touchpad and cursor and performs a tap only when the user taps the touchpad.

The accessibility service does not read, collect, store, sell, or share screen text, passwords, messages, contacts, payment details, browsing content, or other personal content. It is configured not to retrieve window content. Users can decline the disclosure or disable the service at any time in Android Accessibility settings.

Information and Local Data

Dev Jang does not operate a server that collects names, email addresses, phone numbers, precise location, contacts, files, screen content, or touchpad activity from One-Hand Cursor. App settings remain on the device and can be removed by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

The app does not include developer analytics or Firebase Analytics. Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may process device or other identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, advertising data, diagnostics, and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve their services. Processing depends on the user's region, device settings, and consent choices.

Ads and Consent

One-Hand Cursor displays advertising through Google AdMob. Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before loading ads and to provide privacy choices. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; non-personalized or limited ads may still be shown where permitted.

For more information, see Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

The accessibility service permission is used only for the cursor function described above. Overlay permission allows the floating touchpad and cursor to appear above other apps. Internet and network state access are used for ads and consent information. The app does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS, phone, calendar, or media-library access.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Dev Jang through the Google Play listing for One-Hand Cursor.
