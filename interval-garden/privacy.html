Interval Garden Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Interval Garden (package com.evmodu.intervalgarden) works without an account or network connection. This release does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Completed-set count, daily streak, and last-practice date are stored only in local app storage. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may delete this information.

Audio

The app plays bundled audio files. It does not request microphone access, read user audio, or upload recordings.

Services

This release contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, cloud-sync, or payment service.
