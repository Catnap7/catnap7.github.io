Evmodu Apps Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

This policy applies to Android applications published by Evmodu or Catnap7 that link to this page. An app-specific policy takes precedence when one is provided in that app or its Google Play listing.

Overview

Our apps are designed to work primarily on your device. They generally do not require an account, and we do not operate a server that receives the content you enter into these apps unless an app clearly tells you otherwise.

On-device data

Preferences, calculations, notes, history, saved items, and other app content may be stored locally on your device. This local data remains on the device unless you choose to export, share, back up, or otherwise send it using a feature of your device or the app. You can normally remove local app data by clearing storage or uninstalling the app.

Advertising and consent

Apps covered by this policy may display advertising through Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process device or other identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, advertising data, diagnostics, and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve their services. Processing depends on your region, device settings, and consent choices. See Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy for more information.

Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for consent before requesting ads. A privacy choices entry is shown in the app when Google requires one, allowing you to review or withdraw your choices. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; non-personalized or limited ads may still be shown where permitted.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access may be used to load ads and obtain consent information. Some apps may request additional permissions needed for their stated feature. Permission requests are presented by Android, and you can review or revoke them in system settings.

Data sharing and sale

We do not sell personal information. Advertising-related data may be processed by Google and its advertising partners as described above. Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to protect users, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Retention and security

Local app data is retained until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall the app. Google and its partners control retention for data they process under their own policies. We use reasonable safeguards, but no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Apps covered by this policy are intended for a general audience and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You may manage Android permissions and advertising settings through your device, clear or uninstall an app to remove local data, and use the app's privacy choices entry when it is available. You may also contact us about a privacy request using the developer contact shown on the applicable Google Play listing.

Changes

We may update this policy when an app, advertising service, or legal requirement changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the developer through the Google Play listing for the applicable app.
