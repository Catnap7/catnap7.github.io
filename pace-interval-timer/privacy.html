Pace Interval Timer privacy policy

Updated: November 11, 2026

Pace Interval Timer is provided by Evmodu. This policy describes how the Android app handles information.

Timer information

You do not need an account. The app stores preset names, interval durations, rounds, sound and vibration preferences, and the state of a current or interrupted timer in its app storage. The app does not send those timer records to a developer-operated server. Depending on your Android device and account settings, Android backup or device transfer may copy app data separately.

Advertising and privacy choices

The app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Depending on settings and applicable choices, Google's SDK may collect or share information such as IP address, device or account identifiers, interactions with apps and ads, and diagnostics for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. An IP address can indicate an approximate location. Google and its advertising partners process data under their own policies.

The app requests updated privacy information at launch and requests ads only when the consent SDK permits it. When required and available, the Privacy options control in the app lets you review your choices. You can also manage your advertising ID in Android settings. Timer use does not require an ad to load.

Notifications and device features

The app uses notifications and a user-started foreground service to show a running workout timer and continue timing with the screen off. It uses a wake lock while a timer runs, and may play audio or vibrate when you enable those cues. Notification content can appear on your lock screen depending on your device settings. You can change notifications in Android settings.

Deletion and retention

You can delete saved presets in the app or clear the app's data in Android settings. Uninstalling normally removes data stored by the app on that device; separate Android backups may remain under your device and account settings. Google and its advertising partners manage retention and deletion of data they process as described in their own privacy information.
