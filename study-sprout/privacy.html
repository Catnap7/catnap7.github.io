Study Sprout Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Study Sprout (package com.evmodu.studysprout) is a local homework-planning app. It does not create an account, send information to a server, sell data, or share personal information with third parties.

Information stored on your device

The app stores tasks you create, including task title, subject, due date, and completion status, only in local app storage so it can show your plan after restart. Deleting the app removes its locally stored app data according to normal Android behavior.

Services

This release does not use analytics and contains no advertising.
