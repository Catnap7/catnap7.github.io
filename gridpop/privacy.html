GridPop privacy notice

Effective date: October 1, 2026.

GridPop processes selected photos on the device. It does not upload photos, require accounts, or include analytics or advertising SDKs in this version.

Selected photos are copied into private app storage to restore the current editing draft. Layout, crop, color and caption settings are stored with that draft. Clearing the draft removes its private photo copies. Uninstalling removes app-private data. App backup is disabled; your draft does not synchronize across devices.

Saving writes a PNG to Pictures/GridPop. Gallery exports persist independently of the draft and can be deleted through your gallery. Sharing passes an exported image to an app you choose using Android's share sheet; that app's own privacy terms apply. Temporary share images older than one day are removed the next time another image is shared; uninstall also removes them.

No broad photo-library permission, location, contacts, camera or microphone access is required. Only selected photos are opened. Your chosen picker provider may download a photo if you select cloud-backed media; GridPop itself does not upload it.
