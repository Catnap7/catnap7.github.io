Plant Spacing Quick Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 28, 2026

Overview

Plant Spacing Quick (Android package com.evmodu.plant_spacing_quick) is published on Google Play by Dev Jang. It is a local-first utility for estimating garden bed rows, plant counts, spacing, buying quantity, and simple cost totals. The app does not require an account and does not ask you to submit personal content to the developer.

Information We Collect

The developer does not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, location, contacts, photos, files, or garden estimate inputs on a developer server.

Calculator values and recent planting summaries may be stored locally on your device so you can reuse them later. This local data is not sent to Dev Jang servers.

Google Mobile Ads and its advertising partners may process device or other identifiers, approximate location, app interactions, advertising data, diagnostics, and performance data to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and improve their services. Processing depends on your region, device settings, and consent choices.

Ads

Plant Spacing Quick displays advertising through Google AdMob. Where required, the app uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request consent before loading ads and to provide privacy choices. Refusing personalized advertising does not necessarily remove all ads; non-personalized or limited ads may still be shown where permitted.

For more information, see Google's advertising policies and Google's Privacy Policy.

Permissions

Internet and network-state access are used to load ads and obtain consent information. Plant Spacing Quick does not request camera, microphone, contacts, precise location, SMS, phone, calendar, or storage/media permissions.

Data Deletion

Because Plant Spacing Quick does not create an account or store your estimates on a developer server, there is no server-side account data to delete. You can remove local data by clearing app storage or uninstalling the app.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Dev Jang through the Google Play listing for Plant Spacing Quick.
