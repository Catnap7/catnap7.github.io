TextPort Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 3, 2026. Publisher: Dev Jang (Evmodu). Applies to TextPort: Image to Text, Android package com.evmodu.textport.

Photos and recognized text

TextPort uses bundled Google ML Kit models to recognize printed text on your device. The application does not upload selected photos, camera images, or recognized text to our server. We do not operate an OCR server and do not require an account.

You choose images with Android's system picker or a camera app. Working image copies may remain temporarily in TextPort's private cache. Saved notes and settings remain in private app storage. Android app backups are disabled. Your original gallery photos are not deleted by TextPort.

Sharing, export and deletion

Copy, Share and TXT export send the text only to the clipboard, destination app or file location you choose. Those destinations and your keyboard follow their own policies. Delete removes a note from the app; Undo restores it during the displayed undo period. Clear app storage or uninstall to remove TextPort's local notes, settings and cache. Exported files and original photos remain in the locations you selected.

SDK technical data and advertising

Google ML Kit may send installation identifiers, device and app information, recognition configuration, sizes, performance and error events to Google for diagnostics and usage measurement. Photo pixels and recognized text are processed locally. ML Kit data disclosure.

TextPort may show one native advertisement in History. Google's advertising SDK may process IP-based approximate location, advertising or app identifiers, app/ad interactions and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, and share these with advertising partners. SDK network transfers use encrypted transport. Google and its partners determine retention of their data. Mobile Ads data disclosure and Google Privacy Policy.

Where required, Google's consent interface appears before ad requests. Privacy & Ad Choices in Settings lets you revisit choices when an interface is available. Non-personalized or limited ads may still appear when permitted. Refusing consent does not block text recognition. You can also manage advertising identifiers in Android settings. TextPort has no separate developer analytics SDK, purchases or backend account.

Children and contact

TextPort is designed for adults and is not directed to children. For privacy questions, contact devjang.apps@gmail.com. Do not send sensitive document contents when requesting support. We may update this policy when app behavior changes; the effective date will identify the revision.
