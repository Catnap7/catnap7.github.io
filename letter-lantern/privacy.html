Letter Lantern Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 29, 2026

Letter Lantern (package com.evmodu.letterlantern) works without an account or network connection. This release does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

Daily completion date, streak, selected words, language preference, theme preference, and the current free-play puzzle seed are stored only in local app storage. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app may delete this information.

Services

This release contains no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, account, cloud-sync, or payment service.
